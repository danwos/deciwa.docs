Demo¶

There is nothing to install: deciwa already runs on these docs. Every page here is the demo – give feedback on any text, requirement or picture you see.

You start it with the round deciwa logo at the bottom right. Hover it (on a phone: tap it) and switch on Review. From then on every element you point at gets an outline and a click opens its feedback card.

Your feedback stays in this browser only – nobody else sees it, and it survives reloads and page changes. Im/Export in the panel downloads it as a file or resets everything.

A first round¶

Everything happens on this page – the example requirements are right below the steps.

  1. Switch on review – hover the deciwa logo at the bottom right and turn on Review.

  2. Approve a requirement – click The machine is ready for th... (REQ_DEMO_START) and press Approve (key a). The bar beside it and the counter on the logo change.

  3. Reject another one – click A cup of coffee is ready in... (REQ_DEMO_SPEED), write in the comment what is wrong with it and press Reject (key r).

  4. Rate a text – hover this list or any paragraph and give it stars with the keys 0–5.

  5. Select a few words – mark a few words in The machine warns when the ... (REQ_DEMO_WATER) and give feedback on just those.

  6. Look at the panel – switch on Panel: This page shows how much of the page is reviewed and what is still open.

  7. Run a guided review – press ▶ Guided review in the panel (or v): the card jumps from one element without a verdict to the next.

  8. Take it with you – export your review under Im/Export and look at it with Show.
