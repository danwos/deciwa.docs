deciwa¶

decision watcher – point at any part of a doc, a website or a source file and say what you think of it. deciwa keeps your verdict tied to exactly that spot, notices when it changes and shows at any time what is confirmed, what is still open and what must not be touched.

Why deciwa¶

Feedback usually ends up in chats, tickets and comment threads, far away from the text it is about. A week later nobody knows which wording was agreed on, and after the next edit nobody notices that an agreed sentence quietly changed. deciwa puts the feedback where it belongs – on the element itself – and keeps watching it.

  • Feedback stays at the element. A verdict on a paragraph, a requirement or a single word stays with it, even when the text moves.

  • Changes do not slip through. An approved text that is edited later shows up as stale, with old and new wording side by side. A frozen text that changes raises an alarm.

  • Decisions you can see. An approved statement is the decision – no extra list to keep in sync.

  • The AI asks instead of guessing. Claude puts its questions right at the element; you answer with yes, no or a few words.

  • You know where you stand. Per page and for the whole project, deciwa shows how much of what must be approved is approved.

  • Your data stays yours. Reviews live in an open format inside your repository and are versioned with Git.

What you can do¶

Give feedback

approve, reject, approve & freeze or delete – one key or click, with an optional comment – Three ways of working

Rate quality

zero to five stars, independent of any verdict, to find the weak spots

Answer the AI

questions from Claude appear at the element they are about

Review anything you can point at

requirements, headings, paragraphs, table rows, pictures, a selected word – no markers in the source – What you can review

Decide what must be approved

every requirement, every heading, only some pages – What must be approved

Work through a page

guided review jumps from one open element to the next – The review UI

Keep the overview

the panel lists all feedback, open jobs and coverage per page and project

How a review goes¶

  1. Open the result – the built docs page, the website, the file in your editor – and switch review on.

  2. Point and decide – hover an element, press a key: approve, reject, approve & freeze, delete; add stars and a comment if you like.

  3. Let the work happen – an AI agent or a developer picks up the open jobs, changes the text and marks them as done.

  4. See what changed – changed approvals come back as stale, a changed frozen text as an alarm.

  5. Track coverage – watch the share of approved elements grow until the page or the whole project is through.

deciwa is a review and decision tool for everyone who works with docs and code (deciwa is a review and deci... (DEC_PURPOSE)). It collects, evaluates and shows; it does not watch that decisions are kept (deciwa collects, evaluates ... (DEC_NO_ENFORCING)). The change itself is made by the AI or the developer – deciwa makes sure nobody loses track of it.
